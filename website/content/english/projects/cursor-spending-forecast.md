Turn **Spending** tab percents into daily pace, cycle forecast, and stop lines - no API.

From **[Cursor spending](https://cursor.com/dashboard/spending)**:

- **Reset date** - **Current Plan** card: “Usage limits reset on …”
- **Cursor Models %** and **Other Models %** - **Included in Pro** bars
- **Grok reset** and **weekly %** - **Grok Bot** section (when Cursor shows it)

Monthly cycle length comes from the reset date. **On-Demand Usage** is separate; this page tracks plan buckets only.
