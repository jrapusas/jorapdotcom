I've been following Alex Eala's career since her run at the **2025 Miami Open**.
Watching a Filipina rise through the WTA ranks in real time is something I never thought I'd get to do in my lifetime.
I'm glad I started keeping notes when I did.
This page is my running scrapbook for it.
I update it when something happens worth pinning, not on a schedule.
