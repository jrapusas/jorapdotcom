## Transition

- [[Builder to Multiplier]] - your win is team output, not your keyboard
- [[Output vs Outcome]] - shipped feature is not the same as solved problem
- [[Ambiguity Is the Job]] - decide with incomplete facts instead of waiting for certainty
- [[Technical Enough]] - spot bad estimates without competing for the keyboard
- [[Let the Team Own How]] - own what, why, and when - not implementation

## Scope and priority

- [[Scope Is a Promise]] - every yes is a no somewhere else
- [[Say No to Protect the Yes]] - protect the one thing that must land
- [[Iron Triangle]] - scope, time, resources - name what you sacrifice
- [[Cost of Delay]] - waiting can cost more than imperfect ship
- [[Activity Without Accomplishment]] - busy team is not progress
- [[Law of Priorities]] - activity is not accomplishment

## Planning

- [[Steering With a Chart]] - map risks and route before the public yes
- [[Steering Without Chart]] - failure mode when motion replaces a plan
- [[Risk Register]] - top risks named before kickoff
- [[Dependency Mapping]] - who blocks whom
- [[Definition of Done]] - shared proof work is finished
- [[Change Window]] - when scope can still move without breaking trust
- [[Count the Cost]] - decide before you build
- [[Quarterly Planning]] - ninety-day bets, not ninety features

## People and communication

- [[Stakeholder Mapping]] - who cares, who decides, who blocks
- [[Managing Up]] - boss needs signal early, not Friday surprise
- [[Status Without Theater]] - green, yellow, red with facts
- [[RACI]] - one accountable person per decision
- [[Alignment Before Build]] - room agreement beats sprint-four rework
- [[Psychological Safety]] - truth travels when punishment theater is off
- [[Surface Bad News Early]] - name the slip while rollback is cheap
- [[Plain Commitments at Work]] - one owner, one date, one plain yes or no

## Execution rhythm

- [[Weekly Planning Rhythm]] - same plan-and-review cadence every week
- [[One Owner Per Commitment]] - we is not an owner
- [[Meetings Need an Output]] - no recap, owners, and dates means wasted hour
- [[Complete the Cycle]] - owners, dates, verification
- [[Execution]] - gap between deciding and delivered
