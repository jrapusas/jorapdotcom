Your head is for thinking, not for storing everything. A **second brain** is just notes outside your head. You write an idea down once. You find it later when you need it again.

That is what `/notes/` is. Not the blog. **One note = one idea.** Keep it short. Say it in plain words.

Notes link to other notes. That is how you remember. Follow a link on any page. Check **backlinks** to see who pointed here. Open the **[graph view](/notes/graph/)** when you want to see the whole map.

**New here?** Start with [[Getting Started]]. Try [[Reading the Garden]] if you want to read these like a friend shared them, not like a textbook.

Ideas get stronger when they connect. Link a work note to a faith note and a new pattern can show up. Link two notes and a third thought often appears. That is why each idea gets its own page, so it can meet others.

A link is not just a jump. It shows how two ideas relate. **Backlinks** show who pointed here. The **[graph view](/notes/graph/)** shows ideas you might have missed alone. More links usually mean more new thoughts.

Some notes are still rough. That is fine. A messy note with good links beats a perfect note that sits alone. See [[Digital Garden]] and [[Building a Second Brain]] when you want to go deeper.
