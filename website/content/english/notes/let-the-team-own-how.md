  - Let the team own how means you set outcome, constraints, and date - not implementation steps in the standup.
  - [[Builder to Multiplier]] stops personal heroics; this note stops disguised tech lead work with a PM title.
  - Micromanaged how kills ownership and makes you the bottleneck when scale matters.
level_1: "Let the team own how means you own what, why, and when - the builders own implementation."
level_2: "Like a client who names the deadline and budget but not every brush stroke - outcome clear, craft theirs."
level_3: "In the next planning ticket, write acceptance criteria and constraints - leave solution shape to the engineer who will maintain it."
level_4: "[[Technical Enough]] lets you challenge a risky how without prescribing it; [[Know Your Lane]] when you drift into architecture reviews that are not yours."
level_5: "Use [[Definition of Done]] and [[Alignment Before Build]] on the what; reserve how questions for risk reviews only."
