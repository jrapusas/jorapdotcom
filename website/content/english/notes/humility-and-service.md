  - Service is the path to kingdom reward and greatness before God - not the price of [[Free Grace]].
  - [[Faithful Steward]] is the parable shape: well done for faithful use of what He entrusted.
  - [[Law of Addition]] and [[Law of the Picture]] are scripture case studies for the same inverted ladder in [[21 Laws of Leadership in the Bible]].
  - Horizontal echo: [[Leadership]] - direction, pace, and care when people look to you.
