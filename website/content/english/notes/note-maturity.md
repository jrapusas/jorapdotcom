key_concept: |
  - Match polish to stage - a seedling is not an evergreen, and forcing both speeds wastes time.
  - Seedling: raw capture, tagged, dated - enough to find later.
  - Budding: idea survived a second read; links start forming.
  - Evergreen: claim you would cite next year; typed relationships wired.
  - Archive: season ended; keep the record, stop daily maintenance.
  - [[The Knowledge Lifecycle]] names capture through fade; [[Evergreen vs Fleeting Notes]] is the two-speed fork at the start.
level_1: "Note maturity is the stage a note sits in - seedling, budding, evergreen, or archive - and effort should match that stage."
level_2: "Like a plant tray, garden bed, and compost pile - same garden, different jobs at each stage."
level_3: "Tag a new capture as seedling; promote to budding only when you would open it twice in one month."
level_4: "Polishing every seedling burns time notes never repay - archive without shame when the project closed and the idea did not return."
level_5: "Build one promotion rule with [[Promote on Reuse]] and [[Periodic Knowledge Review]] - stages stay honest when the calendar asks."
