level_1: "Risk register is a list of what could break the plan - named before kickoff with owner and mitigation."
level_2: "Like a pre-flight checklist - you read it when calm so you do not invent it mid-storm."
level_3: "Write five risks, who owns watching each, and one mitigation step - review in fifteen minutes at weekly rhythm."
level_4: "[[Fail on Paper First]] imagines failure; register names it while you can still steer - pairs with [[Rollback Principle]]."
level_5: "Keep the register on one page in the project wiki - update at [[Weekly Planning Rhythm]], not only at launch."
